Short bio

Ivan Titov is a Professor of Natural Language Processing at the University of Edinburgh and the University of Amsterdam. At Edinburgh he directs the Centre for Doctoral Training (CDT) in NLP and the Edinburgh ELLIS Unit; he is an ELLIS Fellow, a former Turing Fellow, co-director of the ELLIS NLP programme, and an investigator in SOFAIR, a UKRI-funded national lab for fundamental AI research. His research aims to make language models trustworthy, interpretable, and controllable, with recent work on sparsity and modularity, knowledge editing and unlearning, and interpretability. His work has been funded by an ERC grant and an NWO Vici and recognised with paper awards at ACL and EMNLP. He was Program Co-Chair of ICLR 2021 and CoNLL 2018, and an Action Editor for TACL and JMLR.
