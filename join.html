Joining the lab

Opportunities at Edinburgh and Amsterdam. See the lab page for current and past members.

PhD students

Recruiting

I am looking for motivated PhD students with strong backgrounds in machine learning, NLP, or related areas. You can apply via the ILCC PhD programme, the CDT in Responsible NLP, or the CDT in Machine Learning Systems.

I look for strong technical skills, curiosity, and clear scientific communication. If you would like to reach out before applying, please include a brief description of your research interests and why you think we would be a good fit.

Postdocs

Own funding required

There are no funded postdoc positions at the moment. I am open to hosting postdoctoral researchers on their own fellowships or funding whose interests are closely aligned with the lab's work.

MSc students

Open

We supervise MSc theses for students already enrolled in MSc programmes at Edinburgh or Amsterdam. Unfortunately, we cannot host visiting MSc students from other universities. To inquire, email me with your CV, transcripts, and grades. Recent papers and preprints from work led by MSc students:

  • Knowledge Offloading: Decomposing LLMs into Sparse Backbones and Memory Modules
  • Shared Doubt: Zero-shot Cross-Lingual Confidence Estimation for Language Models (EMNLP 2026)

Visiting PhD students

Own funding required

The lab often hosts visiting PhD students at Edinburgh. Edinburgh is a vibrant environment with a strong NLP and ML community, and visits tend to be very productive. We ask that visitors' research interests are primarily aligned with the lab's work, and that they arrange their own funding before reaching out.

Currently not available

I am not accepting BSc or MInf thesis students in 2026/27.

Undergraduate visitors
